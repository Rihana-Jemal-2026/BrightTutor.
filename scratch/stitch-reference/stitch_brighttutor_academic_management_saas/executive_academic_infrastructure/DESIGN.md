## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, enterprise-grade EdTech aesthetic tailored for academic institutions, enterprise tutoring organizations, and executive administrative teams. It moves decisively away from juvenile, playful learning paradigms, adopting the disciplined precision, structural restraint, and density of platforms like Linear, Stripe Dashboard, and Vercel.

The visual direction is rooted in **Corporate Minimalism** merged with crisp utility:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, high-velocity, rigorous, and intelligent.
- **Emotional Intent:** Instill institutional confidence, operational control, and effortless handling of complex pedagogical workflows.
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous whitespace anchored by razor-sharp border structures, micro-interactions with immediate tactile feedback, rich slate contrast hierarchies, and purposeful emerald accents that signal achievement and institutional vitality without visual noise.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged operational focus, high data density, and clear status triage:

- **Canvas & Surface Architecture:**
  - Base Application Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Secondary Inset Surface: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Navigation / Executive Shell: `#0F172A` (Deep Slate 900) for high-contrast context isolation.

- **Brand & Accent System:**
  - Primary Brand Anchor: `#047857` (Deep Emerald) — conveys stability, authority, and verified state.
  - Interactive Accent: `#10B981` (Vivid Emerald) — used for active indicators, focus rings, and high-priority primary CTAs.
  - Executive Foundation: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) — applied to primary typography, dark navigation panels, and structural contrast.

- **Semantic Triage & Badges:**
  - Success: Fill `#16A34A`, Badge Surface `#DCFCE7`, Badge Border `#86EFAC`
  - Warning: Fill `#D97706`, Badge Surface `#FEF3C7`, Badge Border `#FDE68A`
  - Danger: Fill `#DC2626`, Badge Surface `#FEE2E2`, Badge Border `#FCA5A5`
  - Info: Fill `#0284C7`, Badge Surface `#E0F2FE`, Badge Border `#BAE6FD`

- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - Subtle Border: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
  - High-Contrast Border: `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300)
  - Active/Selection Border: `#047857`

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric editorial confidence with micro-level utilitarian legibility:
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Carries modern architectural structure with tightened letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver authoritative, clear page hierarchy.
- **Body & Data Displays (Inter):** Deployed for all content tiers, complex tabular data, interactive controls, and nested forms. Features optical neutrality and uniform stroke behavior.
- **Technical & Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for institutional IDs, time stamps, grade tracking codes, and operational telemetry.
- **Type Scale Rules:** Maintain strict 14px base sizing for enterprise density. Large displays compress gracefully on mobile viewports while maintaining structural contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-rhythm for tight input, metric, and table alignments.

- **Workspace Framing:**
  - Dark Persistent Executive Shell: A collapsible sidebar (`16.25rem` expanded, `4.5rem` collapsed) utilizing `#0F172A` with contrasting icons and muted navigation text.
  - Main Administrative Canvas: Spans dynamically using a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `1600px`.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: Full 12-column layout, 32px gutters, expanded persistent side navigation, unified action toolbars.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1279px)`: 8-column layout, 24px gutters, automatically collapes sidebar into icon rail, secondary detail drawers fold beneath main panels.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4-column layout, 16px horizontal page margins, sheet-based navigation from bottom, action bars anchor persistently to bottom viewport.
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Cards enforce a 20px (`1.25rem`) or 24px (`1.5rem`) internal padding token.
  - Dense operational tables use tight vertical cell spacing (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`) to maximize visible records above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **low-contrast outlines** complemented by **subtle ambient shadows**, deliberately avoiding loud layered drop shadows or glassy blurs.

- **Border Hierarchy:** Depth is created primarily through structural boundaries. Every surface card and detached panel features a `1px` border in `#E2E8F0` (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`).
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - Level 0 (Flat/Base): `none`. Surface sits integrated directly on `#F8FAFC`.
  - Level 1 (Resting Card): `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Segmented Tabs): `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - Level 3 (Flyouts, Popovers, Dropdowns): `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - Level 4 (Executive Modals & Drawers): `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a backdrop tint of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)`.
- **Active State Depths:** Primary inputs and cards receive zero blur jumps on interaction; instead, they transition cleanly to a dual ring: an inner `1px` border in `#10B981` surrounded by a `3px` focus halo in `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, micro-calibrated shape hierarchy to separate structural canvases from discrete operational widgets:

- **Structural Containers & Dashboard Cards:** Bound to `10px` or `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), creating a clean, modern boundary that avoids excessive softness while steering clear of brutalist sharpness.
- **Controls & Form Fields:** Strict `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner rounding on all buttons, select menus, search inputs, calendar date-pickers, and floating segment selectors.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Fixed `20px` pill-shaped radius (`rounded-full`), providing instant visual distinction between functional buttons and passive institutional meta-data.
- **Avatars & Identity Nodes:** Tutors and institutional personnel receive `8px` rounded squares with a subtle `1px` border; verified student profiles retain circular `rounded-full` treatment for quick scanning in rosters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#047857`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, height `38px` (compact) or `42px` (standard). Hover state transitions to `#065F46` with an inner border highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus ring `3px` in `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Action Icon:** Background `transparent`, text `#64748B`, hover background `#F1F5F9`, hover text `#0F172A`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#DC2626`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`. Active hover transitions to `#DC2626` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Height `38px` for data density; background `#FFFFFF`; border `1px solid #CBD5E1`; radius `8px`; padding `0 12px`.
- Placeholder text `#94A3B8`. Active focus triggers border `#047857` with box shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.12)`.
- Includes integrated left icon slots (16px stroke) for contextual search and date filtering.

### Pill Badges & Status Chips
- Height `22px` to `24px`; radius `20px`; padding `2px 10px`; font-size `11px`; font-weight `600`; uppercase tracking `0.04em`.
- **Tokens:**
  - *Active/Enrolled:* Surface `#DCFCE7`, text `#16A34A`, border `#86EFAC`.
  - *Pending/Under Review:* Surface `#FEF3C7`, text `#D97706`, border `#FDE68A`.
  - *At Risk/Suspended:* Surface `#FEE2E2`, text `#DC2626`, border `#FCA5A5`.
  - *System/Informational:* Surface `#E0F2FE`, text `#0284C7`, border `#BAE6FD`.

### Cards & Dashboards Paneling
- Background `#FFFFFF`; radius `12px`; border `1px solid #E2E8F0`; ambient shadow Level 1.
- Standard card header includes 16px bottom margin with bold title (`title-sm`), muted sublabel, and right-aligned metric or action menu.

### Academic Rosters & Data Grids
- Header row with `#F8FAFC` fill, uppercase `11px` label style in `#64748B`, and `1px solid #E2E8F0` bottom boundary.
- Row items height `48px`, background transitions to `#F8FAFC` on hover. Selected rows feature `#F0FDF4` background and `#047857` indicator strip along the left border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `16px x 16px`; radius `4px` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio); border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked state fills `#047857` with an inner white geometric checkmark or white center dot.

### Enterprise Tutor Metric KPI Cards
- Metric values styled with `display-lg` Plus Jakarta Sans; contextual trend pill embedded in top-right corner; secondary comparison text in `body-sm` `#64748B`.
- Integrated sparklines execute in single-weight `2px` stroke using `#10B981` over a clean zero-fill background.
